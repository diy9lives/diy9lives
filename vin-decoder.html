About VIN decoding
🔍
Data comes from NHTSA's free public vPIC database — the same source used by dealers, insurers, and DMVs in the US.
🛈
Find your VIN on the dashboard (visible through the windshield), the driver's door jamb sticker, or your registration/insurance card.
⚠
This only decodes factory specs. It won't show accident history, title status, or odometer readings — use a vehicle history service for that.
