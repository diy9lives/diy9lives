How it works

For each product, the unit price is the price divided by the quantity. The product with the lowest unit price is highlighted as the best value. For a fair comparison, every product must use the same unit, such as ounces, pounds, or count.

Frequently asked questions

Is the biggest package always the cheapest?

No. Larger packages are often cheaper per unit, but not always. Comparing the unit price is the only reliable way to know.

Do store shelf labels already show unit price?

Often, but different brands sometimes use different units, such as per ounce versus per pound. This tool lets you put them on the same unit.

Is the cheapest per unit always the best deal?

Not if you won't use it before it expires or lack the space to store it. Waste can erase the savings.

How do I compare different units?

Convert everything to one unit first, for example 2 pounds to 32 ounces, and then enter the quantities.
