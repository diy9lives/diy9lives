How it works

Gallons per month equal miles driven divided by your MPG. Monthly fuel cost is gallons times the gas price, and the yearly figure is twelve months of that. Cost per mile is the gas price divided by MPG. The comparison runs the same miles and gas price through the second vehicle's MPG and shows the monthly and yearly difference.

Frequently asked questions

Is the MPG on the window sticker accurate?

EPA ratings come from standardized tests. Real-world MPG changes with speed, temperature, load, tire pressure, and driving style, so your own tank-to-tank number is more reliable if you track it.

How can I get better MPG?

Keep your tires properly inflated, accelerate and brake smoothly, remove unneeded weight, and avoid very high highway speeds. Each helps a little and together they add up.

How do I know how many miles I drive per month?

Check your odometer between fill-ups or compare it across a few weeks. Service records and inspection receipts also show yearly mileage.

Does this work for hybrids and diesel cars?

Yes. Enter the MPG and the fuel price. For plug-in hybrids and fully electric cars, try the EV vs. Gas Cost Calculator.
