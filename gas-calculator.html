Gas saving tips that actually work
💨
Tire pressure. Under-inflated tires reduce fuel economy by up to 3%. Check monthly. Most modern cars show tire pressure in the dashboard.
🕑
Cruise control on highways can improve MPG by 7-14% by eliminating the micro-accelerations most drivers do unconsciously.
⛽
GasBuddy app finds the cheapest gas within a few miles of you. Saving $0.20/gallon adds up to $100+ per year.
Shopping for a fuel-efficient car? FuelEconomy.gov has official MPG ratings for every vehicle.
