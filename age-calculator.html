How it works

The tool compares your date of birth with the “calculate as of” date, which defaults to today, and counts whole years, then remaining months and days using the calendar. It also shows total days and hours lived and the time until your next birthday. Because it counts calendar months, the day count can differ slightly depending on the length of the months involved.

Frequently asked questions

When is my next birthday?

The Next Birthday box shows how many days remain until your next birthday, counted from the “calculate as of” date you choose.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the “calculate as of” date to find how old you were, or will be, on any day.

Why do days and months change by a day sometimes?

Months have different lengths, so the same number of days can be one month and a few days in one period and slightly different in another.

Is my birthday stored?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

Using the number

Someone born March 14, 1992, counted on October 29, 2026, is 34 years, 7 months, and 15 days. The year subtraction is 34, October is 7 months after March, and the 29th is 15 days after the 14th, so nothing has to be borrowed. The next birthday is March 14, 2027, which is 136 days after October 29, 2026. The day total from the birthday to October 29, 2026 is 12,647 days.

The FAQ’s off-by-one case is the other shape: a birthday on the 31st counted in a short month. The page borrows from the month rather than inventing a February 31. Use a past “as of” date for a form that asks your age on a closing day. The birthday is not stored.
