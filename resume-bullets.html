Resume bullet tips
📻
Start with action verbs. Led, Built, Grew, Reduced, Launched, Managed, Increased. Never start with "Responsible for".
📊
Add numbers wherever possible. "Managed social media" becomes "Grew Instagram following by 40% in 6 months." Numbers get interviews.
🤖
ATS tip: Mirror exact keywords from the job posting. Applicant tracking systems scan for matches before a human ever sees your resume.
