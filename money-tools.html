Money math,
in the order it matters.

Start with the number that decides the others. Affordability before the mortgage, the mortgage before the renovation, the debt payment before the retirement contribution.

Most money mistakes are order-of-operations mistakes. A payment that fits a good month becomes a problem in a bad one; a 15-year mortgage is cheaper but only if the payment survives a job change. These tools are built to show the trade, not just the answer.

Every calculator runs in the browser, shows its formula, and links the source for any rate or rule it uses. If a number depends on your lender, your state, or your tax situation, the page says so instead of pretending to know.

What these tools cannot see

  • Your lender's fees, points, escrow rules and mortgage insurance quote. Use the Loan Estimate for those.
  • Your full tax return. Salary and freelance tools use simplified brackets and standard assumptions.
  • Your risk tolerance. A payment that fits the math can still be too tight for your life.
  • Future rates, inflation or investment returns. Projections are scenarios, not forecasts.
