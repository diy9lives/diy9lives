How it works

Every unit is converted through grams. One US stick of butter is 113.4 grams, which equals half a cup, 8 tablespoons, 24 teaspoons, and 4 ounces. A cup is 226.8 grams (2 sticks), and a pound is 453.6 grams (4 sticks). Enter an amount in any unit and the tool shows it in all the others at once.

Frequently asked questions

How much is one stick of butter?

One US stick is half a cup, 8 tablespoons, or about 113 grams, and the wrapper usually has tablespoon lines printed on it.

How many sticks are in a cup?

Two sticks make one cup. A standard one-pound box has four sticks, or two cups.

Is European butter different?

It is usually sold in blocks measured by weight, such as 250 grams, rather than in sticks. Grams are the easiest unit for recipes that cross countries.

Does melted butter measure the same?

By weight, yes. By volume it can differ slightly, so for baking, weigh it when possible.

Using the number

Six tablespoons is 6 × 14.175 grams, about 85 grams, which is three-quarters of a stick. You do not need to cut a cup. Two sticks are a cup, 226.8 grams, which is the usual “one cup of butter” in an American recipe. European-style butter is often sold in 250 gram blocks, not sticks. Weigh 113 grams if the recipe means a stick. Packing a 250 gram block into a cup measure and hoping is how the shortbread gets greasy.

Melted butter measured by the cup is a different question. The FAQ says melted and solid do not occupy the same volume in a casual scoop. Weigh it. The gram column on this page is the one to trust.
