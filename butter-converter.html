Quick reference
🧀
1 US stick of butter = 1/2 cup = 8 tablespoons = 113g = 4oz. This is the standard US wrapper marking — if your butter came in stick form, you can just count the printed tablespoon lines instead of measuring.
🍪
A full box of butter (4 sticks) = 1 pound = 2 cups = 454g. This is the standard US retail butter box.
🇼
UK/EU butter is usually sold by weight, not sticks, so grams are the most reliable unit for international recipes. 250g is a common European butter block size.
