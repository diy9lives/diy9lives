How it works

The tool splits both texts into words and finds the longest sequence of words they share, using the same longest-common-subsequence idea behind many diff programs. Words only in the original are shown struck through in red as removed, and words only in the revised text are shown in green as added. Each side is limited to about 3,000 words so it stays fast. Everything runs in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a code diff?

It compares word by word, which suits prose, contracts, and essays, while code diffs usually compare line by line.

Why is there a word limit?

Comparing every possible match takes more time as the text grows. About 3,000 words per side keeps results instant, so compare long documents section by section.

Is the text I paste stored?

No. Both texts stay in your browser and are not uploaded or saved.

Does it detect moved paragraphs?

Not as moves. A paragraph that moved shows as removed in one spot and added in another.
