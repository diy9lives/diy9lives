Compound interest facts
⏱
Rule of 72. Divide 72 by your interest rate to find how many years it takes to double your money. At 7%, money doubles every ~10 years.
📅
Starting 10 years earlier is more powerful than doubling your contributions. Time is the most valuable variable in compound interest.
💰
S&P 500 historical average: ~10% annually before inflation, ~7% after. Not guaranteed, but a useful benchmark for long-term planning.
