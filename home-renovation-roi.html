How it works

Pick a project type and enter what it will cost. Each project type carries an approximate national average share of its cost that buyers pay back at resale, such as 74% for a mid-range kitchen remodel or 91% for a garage door replacement. Value added is your cost times that share, and net gain or loss is the value added minus the cost. You can enter your own percentage with the Custom option. The percentages are flat averages, so the sale timing does not change them.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the percentages come from?

They are approximate national averages of cost recouped at resale for common projects, similar to what annual industry cost-versus-value studies publish. Actual returns vary a lot by region, market conditions, and quality of work.

Which projects tend to pay back the most?

In this tool's data, exterior and replacement projects such as garage doors, entry doors, and roofs recoup the largest share, while additions and major remodels recoup less.

Does ROI include the enjoyment of living with the project?

No. It only estimates resale value. If you plan to stay for many years, comfort and use matter more than the resale percentage.

What is over-improving?

Spending so much that your home becomes worth more than the nicest homes nearby. Buyers rarely pay a premium beyond the neighborhood's price ceiling.
