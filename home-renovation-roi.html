Home improvement ROI facts
🏠
Kitchen and bathrooms drive the most value. Buyers pay a premium for updated kitchens. A mid-range kitchen remodel returns ~74 cents per dollar spent.
💥
Curb appeal is underrated. Garage doors, entry doors, and landscaping consistently return 80-100%+ because buyers form opinions in the first 30 seconds.
⚠
Over-improving is real. If your renovated home would be worth more than the nicest homes on the block, you will never recover the full cost. Know your neighborhood ceiling.
