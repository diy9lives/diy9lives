Car buying tips
💥
20/4/10 rule. Put 20% down, finance no more than 4 years, keep total car costs under 10% of gross income. Most people violate all three.
📈
72-84 month loans are a trap. You will be underwater (owe more than the car is worth) for years. Stick to 48-60 months max.
💰
Negotiate the total price, not the monthly payment. Dealers love monthly payment talk because it hides how much you are really paying.
Shopping for a car? CarGurus and AutoTempest aggregate listings from every dealer so you can see real market prices.
