How this works
💳
Self-employment tax is 15.3% of 92.35% of your net profit (Social Security + Medicare) — this is on top of regular income tax, and it's the part W-2 employees don't see because employers split it with them.
📅
Quarterly estimated payments are due mid-April, June, September, and January. Missing them can trigger IRS underpayment penalties even if you pay in full by April.
⚠
This is an estimate, not tax advice. It uses simplified 2024 federal brackets and the standard deduction. A CPA can find deductions this tool can't account for.
