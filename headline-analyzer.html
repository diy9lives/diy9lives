How it works

This is a simple heuristic scorer, not a prediction. Your headline starts at 30 points. It earns points for a good length of 40 to 65 characters (or partial credit between 25 and 80), for including a power word (such as “proven,” “free,” or “secret”), an emotional word, a number, and for starting with “How to” or asking a question. The score caps at 100 and the checklist shows which factors you hit.

Frequently asked questions

Does a higher score guarantee more clicks?

No. It checks common patterns, but the best headline is one that honestly matches the content and speaks to your readers. Test headlines with real audiences when you can.

What is the ideal headline length?

Around 40 to 65 characters tends to display fully in search results and social previews without being cut off.

What are power words?

Strong, attention-grabbing words such as “proven,” “essential,” or “secret.” Use them only when they are true, since overpromising hurts trust.

Should every headline have a number?

Not necessarily. Lists and numbers often perform well, but plain, specific headlines can too. Use what fits the content.
