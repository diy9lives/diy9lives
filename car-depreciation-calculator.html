How this is calculated
📈
New cars lose the most value in year one — typically around 20%, since the first owner absorbs the "off the lot" hit. Used cars have already taken that loss, so their curve is gentler from the start.
⚠
This is a generic rule-of-thumb curve, not brand-specific. Reliable brands with strong resale reputations (Toyota, Honda, Subaru) depreciate slower than average; luxury brands and EVs often depreciate faster.
🔧
Mileage and condition matter more than age alone. A well-maintained car with average mileage will hold value better than this curve suggests; heavy mileage or a rough condition will fall faster.
