How it works

Words are counted by splitting on whitespace, characters include every character in the box, and sentences are counted by splitting on periods, question marks, and exclamation marks. Paragraphs are blocks separated by line breaks. Reading time assumes 200 words per minute and speaking time assumes 130, and both show at least one minute. Everything updates live as you type and nothing leaves your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time estimated?

It divides your word count by 200, a typical adult silent reading speed. Speaking time uses 130 words per minute, a natural pace for talking.

Does it count characters with or without spaces?

Both. You get the total characters and the count without spaces, because different forms, tweets, and applications use different rules.

Why might my sentence count look off?

Abbreviations like “Dr.” and numbers with decimals contain periods that look like sentence endings, so counts can be slightly high in text that uses many of them.

Is my text saved?

No. It is processed in your browser and is gone when you close the page.
