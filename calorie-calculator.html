Calorie tips that actually work
📈
3,500 calories = 1 pound. A 500 calorie daily deficit creates a 1 lb/week loss. Sustainable is 0.5-1 lb/week. Anything faster loses muscle too.
🥣
Protein is the most important macro. Aim for 0.7-1g per pound of body weight. High protein preserves muscle during a cut and keeps you full longer.
🏃
TDEE changes as you lose weight. Recalculate every 10-15 lbs lost. Your maintenance calories drop as you get lighter.
