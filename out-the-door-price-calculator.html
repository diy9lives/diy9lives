How this is calculated

How it works

The taxable amount is the vehicle price minus your trade-in value, and sales tax is that amount times your tax rate. The out-the-door total is the price minus the trade-in, plus sales tax, the dealer doc fee, and registration and title fees. Choosing a state fills in its statewide base sales tax rate from Tax Foundation figures published for January 2026, and you can edit the rate to include county or city tax.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my dealer's number differ from this?

Local sales taxes, different fee amounts, and how your state treats trade-ins all change the total. Ask the dealer for an itemized out-the-door price and compare each line.

Does a trade-in always reduce the sales tax?

In most states it does, which is how this tool calculates it. A few states tax the full price regardless, so check your state's rule if the trade-in is large.

Are doc fees negotiable?

It depends on the state. Some states cap doc fees, others do not regulate them. Even where they are fixed, you can often negotiate the vehicle price to offset them.

Why do some states show 0%?

Several states have no statewide sales tax, but they may charge other vehicle taxes or fees, or allow local taxes. Enter the actual amounts if you know them.
