How it works

Choose a date, time, and starting zone. The tool works out the exact moment that represents using your browser's built-in time zone database, which includes daylight saving rules, then displays that same moment in each listed city with its local time, date, and offset from UTC. Rows show next day or previous day when a city is on a different calendar date. The Use current time button fills in the present moment in your selected zone.

Frequently asked questions

Is daylight saving time handled?

Yes. The offsets shown are the ones in effect on the date you pick, so the same two cities can have a different gap in January than in July.

What is UTC?

Coordinated Universal Time is the world's standard reference time. Every other time zone is expressed as an offset ahead of or behind UTC.

Why does the date change in some cities?

When it is late evening in one place, it can already be tomorrow elsewhere. The next day and previous day labels flag this.

Why is my city missing?

The list covers major cities across all regions. Pick a city in the same time zone as yours, and your detected zone is added at the top automatically.
