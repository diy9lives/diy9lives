How it works

Fill in five short steps: your info, the client, the project, line items, and terms. The live preview updates as you type. Each line item is quantity × rate. Tax is applied to the subtotal, and the deposit is a percentage of the total including tax, with the rest shown as the balance due on completion.

Print it or save it as a PDF with your browser's print dialog, or copy it as plain text to paste into an email. Everything is built in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I save the proposal as a PDF?

Click Print / Save PDF, then choose “Save as PDF” as the destination in your browser's print window.

Is my client's information stored anywhere?

No. The proposal exists only in your browser and disappears when you close or refresh the tab, so save or copy it first.

How much deposit should I ask for?

Many freelancers ask for 25% to 50% upfront on smaller projects and use milestone payments on larger ones. There is no fixed rule, so pick an amount that protects you without scaring the client off.

What should a proposal include?

A clear scope of work, deliverables, a timeline, the total price with line items, payment terms, and a date the offer stays valid. Spelling these out prevents most misunderstandings later.
