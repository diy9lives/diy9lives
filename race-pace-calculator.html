How it works

Pace per mile is your total time divided by the distance in miles. The presets use the exact certified distances: 3.10686 miles for a 5K, 6.21371 for a 10K, 13.1094 for a half marathon, and 26.2188 for a marathon, and you can type any custom distance. The split table lists the time for each mile at that steady pace, plus a final partial mile, with a running total.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert pace per mile to pace per kilometer?

Divide your minutes per mile by 1.609. For example, 8:00 per mile is about 4:58 per kilometer.

Should I run even splits?

Even pacing is a good baseline. Many runners do better by starting slightly conservatively and finishing faster, called a negative split, especially in longer races.

What is a good pace?

It depends on age, experience, and goals. Compare yourself to your own previous results rather than other runners.

Does this account for hills or heat?

No. It assumes a flat course at constant effort. Hills, heat, and wind slow most runners, so adjust your plan for race conditions.
