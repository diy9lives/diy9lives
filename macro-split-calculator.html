Diet styles explained
⚖
Balanced (30/40/30): A moderate, sustainable split good for general health and most fitness goals.
🥣
High protein (35/35/30): Prioritizes muscle retention and satiety — popular for cutting or recomposition.
🥗
Low carb (30/20/50): Reduces carbs without going full keto — often used for steadier energy and appetite control.
🥚
Keto (25/5/70): Very low carb to encourage ketosis. Fat becomes the dominant fuel source.

How it works

Pick a calorie target and a diet style. Each style assigns a share of calories to protein, carbohydrates, and fat: balanced 30/40/30, high protein 35/35/30, low carb 30/20/50, and keto 25/5/70. Calories are converted to grams using 4 calories per gram for protein and carbs and 9 per gram for fat, so grams = calories × share ÷ 4 (or ÷ 9 for fat). The bar shows the split.

Frequently asked questions

Which macro split is best?

There is no single best split. The right one depends on your goals, preferences, activity level, and health. Consistency over time matters more than the exact percentages.

Is keto safe for everyone?

No. Very low carbohydrate diets are not suitable for everyone, including people with certain medical conditions or on certain medications. Talk to a doctor or dietitian first.

How are grams calculated?

Calories for each macro are divided by its energy density: 4 calories per gram for protein and carbohydrates, and 9 calories per gram for fat.

How is this different from the Calorie Calculator?

The Calorie Calculator estimates how many calories you need. This tool starts from a calorie number you already have and splits it by diet style.
