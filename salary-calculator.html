Salary tips
📈
The 50/30/20 rule: 50% of take-home to needs, 30% to wants, 20% to savings. A simple starting budget for any income level.
💵
Max your 401k match first. A 6% employer match is a 100% return on investment. Never leave free money on the table before spending anywhere else.
🎯
Negotiating salary? Always negotiate. Studies show 85% of people who ask for more get it. The worst they can say is no.
