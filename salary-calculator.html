Salary tips
📈
The 50/30/20 rule: 50% of take-home to needs, 30% to wants, 20% to savings. A simple starting budget for any income level.
💵
Max your 401k match first. A 6% employer match is a 100% return on investment. Never leave free money on the table before spending anywhere else.
💼
Working multiple jobs? Each employer withholds Social Security separately up to the wage cap, so if your combined income exceeds it, you may get some of that back as a credit when you file. This calculator estimates the cap on your combined total, which can differ slightly from actual withholding.
