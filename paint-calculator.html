Pro painting tips
🎨
Always buy 10% extra. Batch variations between cans are real, and you will need touch-up paint later. Better to return a can than run out mid-wall.
✏
Primer is not optional on new drywall or when going from dark to light. Skip it and you will need 3+ coats. With primer, 2 coats is usually enough.
🌐
Flat vs eggshell vs satin: Flat hides imperfections (ceilings, low-traffic). Eggshell is best for most walls. Satin or semi-gloss for bathrooms, kitchens, and trim.
Ready to buy? Home Depot and Lowe's both offer free color matching and will mix any paint color in-store.
