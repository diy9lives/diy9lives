What these tools can and cannot tell you

DIY9lives is a set of free calculators and plain-language guides. They are useful for planning and checking arithmetic. They are not a substitute for a professional who can see your actual situation.

The short version

Every number on this site is an estimate produced from the values you type and the assumptions printed on the page. Check the assumptions before you rely on the result. For anything expensive, regulated, structural, medical or irreversible, confirm the answer with a qualified professional or the official source.

Money, tax and legal

Mortgage, loan, salary, retirement, debt, tax and affordability calculators use simplified formulas and the inputs you provide. They do not know your full tax situation, your lender's fees, your state's rules or your risk tolerance.

Nothing here is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Tax brackets, contribution limits and state rules change; confirm current figures with the IRS, your state revenue department or a licensed professional. A calculator cannot replace a Loan Estimate, a tax return, or an attorney's review of a contract.

Health and fitness

BMI, calorie, pregnancy, cycle, sleep, heart-rate and one-rep-max tools are screening or planning estimates, not diagnoses. They use population formulas that do not account for your medical history, medications, pregnancy, disability or body composition.

Talk to a clinician before changing medication, training, diet or anything related to pregnancy. If you think you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number. Do not delay care because of a number on this site.

Home, construction and cars

Paint, concrete, flooring, mulch, BTU, tire, towing and repair calculators estimate quantities and fitment from the dimensions you enter. Real jobs involve waste, slopes, irregular shapes, local code, substrate condition and tolerances that a calculator cannot see.

Follow manufacturer instructions and local building codes. For structural work, gas, electrical, roofing or anything that could injure someone, use a licensed contractor or engineer. Tire and wheel calculations do not confirm clearance, load rating, speed rating or legality; the vehicle placard and a qualified tire professional are the authority.
