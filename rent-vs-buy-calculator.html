How this is calculated
💰
Cost to buy = down payment + total mortgage payments + taxes + insurance + maintenance − equity built − home appreciation, over your time horizon.
📈
Cost to rent assumes your down payment is invested instead, growing at your chosen return rate — this "opportunity cost" is the key thing simple calculators miss.
⏳
Time horizon matters most. Buying usually wins the longer you stay, since closing costs and early mortgage interest get amortized over more years.
