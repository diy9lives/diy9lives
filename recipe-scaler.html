How it works
The tool reads the number at the start of each ingredient line, including whole numbers, fractions like 1/2, and mixed numbers like 1 1/2, multiplies it by your multiplier, and rewrites the line with the result. Results are shown as practical fractions in halves, thirds, quarters, and eighths so they match real measuring cups and spoons. Lines without a leading number are kept as written. Choosing a desired serving size sets the multiplier to desired servings divided by original servings.


Frequently asked questions
Why are some amounts shown as fractions?
Measuring cups and spoons come in fractions, so scaled amounts are rounded to the nearest practical fraction rather than long decimals.
Can I scale cooking time and pan size too?
Not automatically. Doubling a baked recipe often needs a bigger pan or two pans, and time may change, so follow baking guidance for the dish.
What format should I type ingredients in?
Start each line with the amount, then the unit and ingredient, such as “2 cups flour” or “1 1/2 tsp salt.” Lines without a leading number are left unchanged.
Does this convert between units?
No. It only changes quantities. For converting cups to grams, use our Cooking Measurement Converter.
