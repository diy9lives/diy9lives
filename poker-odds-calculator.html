Essential poker odds to memorize
♠
The Rule of 2 and 4: Multiply your outs by 4 after the flop (before turn), or by 2 after the turn. You get your approximate win percentage. 8 outs to a flush = ~32% on the flop.
💵
Pot odds vs equity: If the pot is $100 and you must call $25, you need 25/125 = 20% equity to break even. Only call if your win % beats the pot odds required.
📈
Starting hand strength (6-max): Premium: AA, KK, QQ, AK. Strong: JJ, TT, AQ, AJ. Playable: 99-77, KQ, AT, KJ. Everything else is situation-dependent.
