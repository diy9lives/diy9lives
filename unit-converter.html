📚 Quick Reference

How it works

Each category (length, weight, temperature, volume, speed, area) stores every unit as a multiple of one base unit, such as meters for length or kilograms for weight. The tool converts your value into the base unit and then into the target unit, so any pair works. Temperature uses formulas instead: F = C × 9/5 + 32, C = (F − 32) × 5/9, and K = C + 273.15. Volume units are US customary, so a gallon here is the US gallon.

Frequently asked questions

Is a US gallon the same as an imperial gallon?

No. A US gallon is about 3.785 liters, while an imperial (UK) gallon is about 4.546 liters. This converter uses US units.

How precise are the results?

The conversion factors are accurate to many decimal places, and results are displayed rounded for readability.

What is the difference between weight and mass?

In everyday use they are interchangeable. Scientifically, mass is the amount of matter and weight is the force of gravity on it. Kilograms measure mass, but most people call them weight.

Why do the units update when I switch categories?

Each category has its own set of units, so the lists change to the ones that make sense for what you are converting.
