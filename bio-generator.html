Enter your name, title, who you help, a top result, and an optional personal touch, then pick a platform. Frequently asked questions

Are these character counts the platforms' official limits?

They are practical target lengths for each platform. Platforms change their limits from time to time, so check the current limit before you paste.

Is the bio written by AI?

No. It uses templates built around your answers, so edit it until it sounds like you.

What makes a good bio?

In a sentence or two, say who you help, what result you deliver, and why someone should trust you. Add one human detail and end with how to reach you.

Should I use the same bio everywhere?

Keep the core message consistent but adapt the length and tone. LinkedIn can be longer and more professional, while X and Instagram need to be short and punchy.
